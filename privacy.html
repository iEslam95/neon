NEON privacy policy

NEON is a personal assistant used only by its owner. It accesses Google data (Gmail, Calendar, YouTube, Drive) only for the account owner who signed in, and only to carry out his own requests.

  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer. Google tokens are stored locally on that computer.
  • No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Emails are only sent, files only shared or deleted, after the owner approves each action.
  • The owner can revoke access at any time from his Google Account permissions page.

Use of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Last updated: November 2026.
